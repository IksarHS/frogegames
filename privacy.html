Privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026

Who we are

Froge, LLC is a small game studio in California, United States. This policy covers frogegames.com (the "Website") and the games we publish. Questions and requests go to dean@frogegames.com.

The Website

When you visit, our service providers may automatically receive technical details such as your IP address, device type, operating system, browser type and the pages you view. We use this information to deliver, secure and improve the Website.

Analytics. We may use analytics services, such as Google Analytics, to understand how people use the Website. These services may set cookies and may receive information about your visits. Google explains how it uses information from sites that use its services. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Our games

Saves, settings and anything you make in a released game stay on your device, and we don't receive them. Deleting the game may delete them, and we can't restore them. Our released games don't have advertising, third-party analytics or tracking across other apps and websites. If a released game ever shares user data with a third party, that party will provide the same or equal protection as this policy.

If you get a game from a store or game platform, it handles your account, download and payment under its own privacy policy. It may give us reports such as sales totals, and crash reports from people who agreed to send them. We use these to run the studio and fix bugs.

A test version we share with invited players can work differently. We tell testers what it collects before they send us anything. A testing service may also show us the name and email address used to invite a tester.

Children

Some of our games are made for young children. Those games don't collect personal information from children. They have no accounts, ads or third-party analytics, and they send nothing to us.

The Website and our email aren't intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you think your child sent us personal information, email us and we'll delete it.

If you contact us

When you email us, we receive your address and whatever you write. We use it to reply and to help with your question or problem.

Who else handles information

We share information with service providers that help us run the Website, our email and our testing, so they can provide those services to us. We may also disclose information to comply with the law, to detect fraud or abuse, or to protect the rights or safety of Froge or others.

We don't sell personal information. Apart from the analytics services described above, we don't allow other parties to collect information about your activity over time and across websites through the Website or our games.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purposes in this policy, unless the law requires or permits longer. Ask us to delete your information and we'll do it within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it. Service providers and analytics services keep their own records under their own policies.

Your choices and rights

You can email us to ask what we hold about you, or to correct or delete it. If you gave us permission to use something, you can withdraw it the same way. The Website does not currently respond to Do Not Track signals.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
